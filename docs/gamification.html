The system that drives engagement through Badges, Medals, and Points.

💎 The Triad of Rewards

Points

Earned from **Tests**. Stored in UserPoint history. Used for Rank logic.

Badges

Earned from **Quizzes**. Visual icons representing skills acquired.

Certifications

Earned by finishing **100% of a Course**. The final diploma.

📊 Performance Ranks

Based on total points, the UserPerformance service calculates the level:

  • Novice 0 - 100 Points
  • Aspirant 101 - 500 Points
  • Expert 501+ Points
